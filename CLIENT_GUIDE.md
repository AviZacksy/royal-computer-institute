# Royal Computer Institute - Client Guide

> [!TIP]
> Welcome to your new Institute Management System! This guide will help you manage your institute effortlessly. You don't need any technical knowledge—just follow the steps below.

---

## 1. Introduction

### What the software does
The Royal Computer Institute Management System is a complete digital solution to run your computer institute. It takes your day-to-day operations—like managing students, collecting fees, conducting exams, and issuing certificates—and puts them into one easy-to-use online platform.

### Main Modules
- **Student Management:** Add, approve, and manage all your students.
- **Fee Management:** Track payments, verify receipts, and check pending dues.
- **Exam Engine:** Create online tests, manage a question bank, and automatically grade students.
- **Documents:** Generate Professional Admit Cards, Marksheets, and Certificates with one click.
- **Website Management:** Update your public gallery and view incoming enquiries from visitors.

### User Roles
1. **Admin (You):** Has full control over everything. Can manage students, fees, exams, and website content.
2. **Student:** Can log in to view their profile, pay fees, download notes, take exams, and download their certificates.

## 2. First Time Setup

When you first receive the software, follow these steps to get everything running smoothly.

### How to login as Admin
1. Go to your Admin Login URL (e.g., `www.yourinstitute.com/admin/login`).
2. Enter your Admin Email and Password provided by the developer.
3. Click **Login**.

### Recommended Order of Setup
To avoid running into issues, set up your institute in this exact order:
1. **Add Courses:** Create the courses you offer (e.g., DCA, PGDCA) and set their total fees.
2. **Add Questions:** Build your question bank so you have questions ready for exams.
3. **Create Exams:** Create exams and assign the questions to them.
4. **Add Students:** Now you can start adding students and assigning them to the courses you created.

## 3. Admin Guide

Here is a step-by-step breakdown of every feature in your Admin Panel.

### Dashboard
- **Purpose:** Gives you a quick overview of your institute (total students, total revenue, pending approvals).
- **Location:** The first page you see after logging in.
- **Usage:** Simply look at the summary cards. Click on any card to view detailed reports.

### Student Management
- **Purpose:** To view, edit, and manage all registered students.
- **Location:** `Sidebar > Students > All Students`
- **Usage:**
  1. Click on **All Students**.
  2. Use the search bar to find a specific student.
  3. Click **View/Edit** to update their details or change their password.
- **Common Mistakes:** Deleting a student instead of deactivating them. If a student leaves, it's better to mark them inactive so you keep their payment history.

### Student Approval
- **Purpose:** When students register from the website, they must be approved before they can log in.
- **Location:** `Sidebar > Students > Pending Approvals`
- **Usage:**
  1. Review the student's submitted details.
  2. Click **Approve** to grant them access, or **Reject** if the application is invalid.
- **What happens next:** Once approved, the student can log in using their email and password.

### Courses
- **Purpose:** Manage the computer courses your institute offers.
- **Location:** `Sidebar > Courses`
- **Usage:**
  1. Click **Add New Course**.
  2. Enter the Course Name, Duration, and Total Fee.
  3. Click **Save**.
- **Common Mistakes:** Changing a course fee later might not affect students who have already been assigned the old fee.

### Fee Management
- **Purpose:** See who has paid and who has pending dues.
- **Location:** `Sidebar > Fees`
- **Usage:** View the list of students and their payment statuses. You can filter by "Paid" or "Pending".

### Payments
- **Purpose:** Verify fee payments made by students.
- **Location:** `Sidebar > Fees > Payments`
- **Usage:**
  1. Find a payment marked as "Pending Verification".
  2. Click to view the uploaded payment screenshot.
  3. If valid, click **Approve Payment**.
- **Common Mistakes:** Approving a payment without actually checking your bank account to confirm the money arrived.

### Receipt Generation
- **Purpose:** Give students a digital or printed receipt for their payments.
- **Location:** `Sidebar > Fees > Receipts`
- **Usage:**
  1. Find an approved payment.
  2. Click **Download Receipt** or **Print**.
- **What happens next:** A PDF receipt is generated instantly.

### Gallery
- **Purpose:** Upload photos of your institute, events, or prize distributions to show on your public website.
- **Location:** `Sidebar > Website > Gallery`
- **Usage:**
  1. Click **Upload Image**.
  2. Select the photo from your computer.
  3. Click **Upload**.
- **Common Mistakes:** Uploading images that are too large (e.g., 10MB). Try to keep images under 2MB for a fast website.

### Enquiries
- **Purpose:** View messages sent by visitors from your website's "Contact Us" page.
- **Location:** `Sidebar > Website > Enquiries`
- **Usage:** Read the messages and use the provided phone number or email to contact the prospective student.

### Question Bank
- **Purpose:** A master list of all multiple-choice questions for your exams.
- **Location:** `Sidebar > Exams > Question Bank`
- **Usage:**
  1. Click **Add Question**.
  2. Type the question, provide 4 options, and select the correct answer.
  3. Click **Save**.

### Exams
- **Purpose:** Create an exam (e.g., "DCA Final Exam").
- **Location:** `Sidebar > Exams > All Exams`
- **Usage:**
  1. Click **Create Exam**.
  2. Set the Title, Course, Duration (in minutes), and Passing Marks.
  3. Click **Save**.

### Assign Questions
- **Purpose:** Add questions from your Question Bank into a specific exam.
- **Location:** Inside a specific Exam's view page.
- **Usage:**
  1. Open an Exam.
  2. Click **Manage Questions**.
  3. Tick the checkboxes next to the questions you want to include.
  4. Click **Save Assigned Questions**.
- **Common Mistakes:** Forgetting to assign questions. If an exam has 0 questions, students will see a blank page.

### Student Attempts
- **Purpose:** See how much a student scored on an exam.
- **Location:** `Sidebar > Exams > Results / Attempts`
- **Usage:** View the list of students who have taken the exam, their score, and whether they passed or failed.

### Documents (Admit Card, Certificate, Marksheet)
- **Purpose:** Generate official institute documents.
- **Location:** `Sidebar > Documents`
- **Usage:**
  1. Select the type of document (e.g., Certificate).
  2. Search for the student.
  3. Ensure their exam marks and details are correct.
  4. Click **Generate PDF**.
- **What happens next:** A beautifully formatted PDF is created, ready for printing.

### Notes
- **Purpose:** Upload study materials (PDFs, PPTs) for students to download.
- **Location:** `Sidebar > Study Material > Notes`
- **Usage:**
  1. Select the Course.
  2. Click **Upload Document**.
  3. Choose the file from your computer.

### Logout
- **Purpose:** Securely exit the admin panel.
- **Location:** Top right corner or bottom of the sidebar.
- **Usage:** Always click **Logout** when leaving your computer to protect your student data.

## 4. Student Guide

If your students ask how to use the portal, you can share this information with them.

### Login
- Students go to the website and click **Student Login**. They enter their registered email and password.

### Dashboard
- The first screen they see. It shows their enrolled course, upcoming exams, and pending fee amount.

### My Fees
- Students can view their fee history. If they need to pay, they can view your institute's UPI QR code, make the payment, and upload the screenshot here for you to verify.

### My Exams
- Shows a list of exams assigned to their course. If an exam is active, they will see a **Start Exam** button.

### Taking an Exam, Timer, Auto Submit
- Once they click **Start Exam**, a timer begins at the top of the screen.
- They must select answers for all questions.
- **Auto Submit:** If the timer runs out (e.g., after 60 minutes), the exam will automatically submit itself, even if they haven't finished.

### Viewing Results
- Immediately after submitting (or when you publish the results, depending on settings), the student can see their total score and Pass/Fail status.

### Downloading Documents & Notes
- Under **My Documents**, students can download their approved Admit Cards or Certificates.
- Under **Study Material**, they can download the PDFs you uploaded for their course.

## 5. Complete Institute Workflow

Here is the ideal, perfect workflow from the moment a new student walks in, to the moment they graduate:

1. **Receive Enquiry:** A student visits the website and submits an enquiry.
2. **Approve Student:** The student registers online. You log in as Admin and **Approve** them.
3. **Assign Course:** During approval (or editing), you assign them to the "DCA" course.
4. **Collect Fees:** The student logs in, sees their fees, pays via UPI, and uploads a screenshot.
5. **Verify Payment:** You log in, check the screenshot against your bank app, and click **Approve Payment**.
6. **Generate Receipt:** You generate a fee receipt and print it for the student.
7. **Upload Notes:** You upload the DCA Chapter 1 PDF notes to the portal.
8. **Create Exam:** You create the "DCA Mid-Term Exam".
9. **Assign Questions:** You select 50 questions from your Question Bank for this exam.
10. **Student takes exam:** The student logs in from home or the institute lab, takes the exam, and the system auto-grades it.
11. **Generate Marksheet:** You generate a professional marksheet based on their score.
12. **Generate Certificate:** Once the course is complete, you generate their final Certificate.

## 6. Supabase Storage (Where your files go)

Your software uses a cloud database called **Supabase**. To keep the system fast, text data and image files are stored differently.

**Text data** (names, phone numbers, scores) is stored in the Database.
**Files** (images, PDFs) are stored in "Storage Buckets".

Here is what each bucket does:
- **`gallery` Bucket:** Stores the images you upload for the public website gallery.
- **`payments` Bucket:** Stores the screenshots students upload when paying their fees.
- **`documents` Bucket:** Stores copies of generated certificates or admit cards (if saved).
- **`notes` Bucket:** Stores the study material (PDFs/Word docs) you upload for students.

> [!NOTE]
> When you delete a student, make sure to also delete any of their files from these buckets if you want to free up cloud storage space.

## 8. FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

### Student Management
1. **How do I add a student?**
   Students can register via the website, and you approve them in `Pending Approvals`. You can also manually add them from `Sidebar > Students > Add Student`.
2. **How do I approve a pending admission?**
   Go to `Pending Approvals`, review their details, and click **Approve**.
3. **Can I delete a student?**
   Yes, but it is highly recommended to "Deactivate" them instead so you don't lose their historical payment data.
4. **What if a student forgets their password?**
   Go to `All Students`, edit their profile, and you can type a new password for them.
5. **How do I assign a course to a student?**
   When editing or approving a student, there is a dropdown menu to select their Course.

### Courses & Fees
6. **How do I change fees?**
   Go to `Courses`, edit the course, and update the fee amount.
7. **Does changing a course fee affect existing students?**
   Usually no. Existing students retain the fee structure they were assigned at admission.
8. **How do I mark fees as paid if they paid in cash?**
   Go to `Fees`, select the student, and manually add a payment record marking the method as "Cash".
9. **How do I verify a payment screenshot?**
   Go to `Payments`, click the pending transaction, view the image, check your bank, and click Approve.
10. **How do I download receipts?**
    Go to `Fees > Receipts`, find the approved payment, and click Download.

### Exams & Questions
11. **How do I create an exam?**
    Go to `Exams > Create Exam`, fill in the title, duration, and course.
12. **How do I add a question to the question bank?**
    Go to `Question Bank > Add Question`.
13. **Can a question have multiple correct answers?**
    Currently, the system is designed for single correct answer multiple-choice questions (MCQs).
14. **How do I assign questions to an exam?**
    Open the Exam, click `Manage Questions`, and tick the ones you want.
15. **Can students see their exam results immediately?**
    Yes, the system auto-grades the exam the moment they submit it.
16. **What happens if an exam timer runs out?**
    The exam automatically submits the answers they have selected so far.
17. **Can a student retake an exam?**
    Yes, if you go to their attempts and delete their previous attempt, or if you increase the allowed attempt limit.

### Documents & Study Material
18. **How do I upload notes?**
    Go to `Notes`, select the course, and upload your PDF.
19. **Where does a student find uploaded notes?**
    In their Student Dashboard under the "Study Material" section.
20. **How do I generate an admit card?**
    Go to `Documents > Admit Card`, select the student and exam, and click generate.
21. **What details are needed for a marksheet?**
    The student must have completed an exam so the system can pull their marks.
22. **How do I generate a certificate?**
    Go to `Documents > Certificate`, select the student, and click generate.
23. **How does a student download their certificate?**
    Once you generate and publish it, it appears in their `My Documents` tab.

### Website Management
24. **How do I upload images to the gallery?**
    Go to `Gallery`, click Upload, and choose your image.
25. **How do I delete a gallery image?**
    Go to `Gallery`, hover over the image, and click the trash/delete icon.
26. **Where do website enquiries go?**
    They appear in your Admin Panel under `Enquiries`.
27. **How do I reply to an enquiry?**
    The system doesn't email them back directly. You use the provided phone number to call them or email them from your personal Gmail.

### General & Security
28. **How do I log out of the admin panel?**
    Click the Logout button in the sidebar or top menu.
29. **Can I have multiple admin accounts?**
    Yes, you can ask your developer to create additional admin accounts if you have staff members.
30. **Is the data secure?**
    Yes, it is securely hosted on Supabase with modern encryption standards.
31. **What happens if I lose my internet connection?**
    You need an active internet connection to use the software. If it drops during an action, simply refresh the page once reconnected.

## 9. Troubleshooting

If something isn't working right, check these common solutions before contacting support:

| Issue | Cause & Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Image won't upload to gallery** | The file size is likely too large. Compress the image to under 2MB and try again. |
| **Student cannot log in** | 1. They are typing the wrong password.<br>2. You haven't "Approved" their account yet.<br>3. Their account is marked "Inactive". |
| **Exam is not showing for student** | 1. The exam is assigned to the wrong Course.<br>2. The exam is marked as "Inactive/Draft". |
| **Receipt PDF is blank/failing** | Ensure the payment has been fully approved first. |
| **"Invalid Credentials" for Admin** | Double-check your spelling and ensure CAPS LOCK is off. |

## 10. Backup Recommendations

Your data is your most valuable asset. While Supabase handles automatic backups, it is highly recommended to practice good data hygiene:

1. **Export Student Lists:** Once a month, if your system has an export feature, download your student list as an Excel/CSV file and save it to your local computer.
2. **Supabase Database Backups:** Your developer can configure Supabase to run daily automated backups. Ensure this feature is active in your Supabase project settings.
3. **Storage Backups:** Files in the `notes`, `gallery`, and `documents` buckets are important. Always keep the original files (like your master PDF notes) on your physical computer's hard drive as well.
4. **Do Not Share Admin Passwords:** Only give admin access to trusted managers. If an employee leaves, immediately change the admin password.
